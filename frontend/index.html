          FAQ

          Common questions

          Is my CV data private and secure?

          Yes. Your CV is processed in-memory only and never stored on our servers. Each session is completely isolated. We do not train on your data or share it with any third parties.

          What file formats does Kairix accept?

          Kairix accepts PDF, DOCX, JPG, PNG, and WEBP files up to 10 MB. For scanned CVs or photos of physical documents, use JPG or PNG — Google Cloud Vision will extract the text automatically.

          How accurate is the AI analysis?

          The analysis is powered by Llama 3.3 70B (via Groq) with a RAG knowledge base of 2025 job market data. It provides highly contextual, role-specific feedback — not generic advice. Results are most accurate when you paste a job description to enable ATS matching.

          Do I need to create an account?

          No account required. Upload your CV, describe your goal, and receive your full intelligence report instantly — for free. You can optionally enter your email to receive the report in your inbox.

          Which browsers support the voice input feature?

          Voice input works in Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge. Firefox has limited support for the Web Speech API. You can always type your career goal manually — the analysis quality is identical either way.
